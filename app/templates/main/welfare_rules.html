  1. Monthly Contribution: K1,000 per month.
  2. Late Payment Penalty: 5% per month on the outstanding contribution.
    Example: 2 months late = 10% penalty → pay K1,100 instead of K1,000
  3. Back Payments: Missed months must be paid for the exact period they were missed.
  4. Benefits Calculation: Months contributed = times eligible for support.
    Example: 6 months contributed = eligible for support up to 6 times
